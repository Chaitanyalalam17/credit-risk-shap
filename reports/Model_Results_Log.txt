OVERFITTING FINDINGS AND MITIGATION:
  All ensemble models showed Train F1=1.000 vs Test F1~0.55
  Root cause: SMOTE synthetic samples too easy to memorise
              on small dataset (n=1,000 training)
  Mitigation: Stronger regularisation applied to XGBoost
              and LightGBM (reg_alpha, reg_lambda, max_depth=3)
  Result: Gap reduced from ~0.43 to ~0.25
  Academic note: This is discussed in Results chapter
                 as a limitation of SMOTE on small datasets
